Features Guide

⚡

Shorts Transcript Extraction

Seamlessly view transcripts for YouTube Shorts. We inject a slick, dark-themed Transcript button right next to the Like/Dislike actions on Shorts. No more struggling to read fast-paced text!

⌨️

Alt + T Shortcut

Press Alt + T on any YouTube video (Shorts or Normal) to instantly toggle the transcript view. It's built for power users who want keyboard-first navigation.

📋

One-Click Copy & Download

In both Shorts and Normal videos, use our injected "Copy Transcript" buttons to instantly copy the entire script to your clipboard, or download it as a .txt file.

🎨

Dark Theme First

Our custom UI elements (like the Shorts transcript popup) are built with modern glassmorphism and deep dark themes to blend perfectly with YouTube's cinematic mode.

User Manual

How to use Shorts Transcripts

  1. Navigate to any YouTube Short.
  2. Look at the right-side vertical panel (where Like, Dislike, and Share are).
  3. Click the new Transcript button.
  4. A popup will appear with the full transcript. Use the Copy or Download buttons inside the popup.

How to use Transcripts on Normal Videos

  1. Open any standard YouTube video.
  2. Press Alt + T on your keyboard.
  3. The native YouTube transcript panel will open on the right side.
  4. Look at the top of the panel for our custom green Copy Transcript ▼ button.
  5. Click it to choose between copying just the text, or the text with timestamps.

Troubleshooting

If buttons are not appearing:

  • Ensure you are not on a very old version of YouTube layout.
  • Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R).
  • Check if another YouTube extension is conflicting with the layout.
